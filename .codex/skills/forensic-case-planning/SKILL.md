# Forensic case planning

Use registered information only. Establish the case ID, authority, scope, examination questions, evidence identifiers, roles, time reference, exclusions, and required review. Mark unavailable facts `unknown` or `not-determined`; never complete them from plausibility.

Select the smallest method that can answer each question. Confirm that its method pack, capability, validation basis, limitations, and required authorization fit the case. Record alternatives and explain why the selected method is proportionate.

Do not treat planning as authorization. Before execution, route the approved bounds through `Approve-NexusMethod` and the runtime enforcement described in [operations](../../../docs/operations.md).
